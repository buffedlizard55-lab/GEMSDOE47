What is H51?

H51 is a multi-scale slope-anomaly persistence detector. It uses the product of the slope anomaly at two regional scales (25 px / 2.5 km and 12 px / 1.2 km) as the detection field. A fault scarp that stands out from both the broad and narrow regional levels scores high; erosional features that only break one scale are suppressed.

This is the multi-scale persistence idea from Frangi filtering (Frangi et al. 1998), applied to the slope anomaly field rather than to the Hessian. No prior GEMSDOE submission used this approach.
