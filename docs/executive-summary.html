Split-conformal spacing audit: assumptions and result

  • Frozen split: five blocks for spacing selection, six separate blocks for calibration, and five locked-test blocks. Spacings 2–6 px were screened. Both H47-B and the single-scale arm selected d=5 px / 500 m using selection blocks only.
  • Calibration sample: six block-level scores; the selected-arm order statistic is rank 6 of 6, giving a nominal one-sided marginal level of 6/7 ≈ 85.7%, conditional on exchangeability.
  • Lower bound: the clipped lower prediction bound is 0.000 for both arms. Thus there is no positive floor even for the proxy target under the stated assumptions; it cannot authorize a slot.
  • Important caveats: calibration scores are against the known-fault catalogue-mask proxy, not the hidden off-catalogue target; spatial block exchangeability is unverified; two calibration blocks have no mask pixels. The level is not missing-fault coverage, cellwise/conditional coverage, or a guarantee of private score, rank, or future geographic regions.

Standard split conformal finite-sample marginal coverage requires exchangeability; it is not assumption-free. See Lei et al. (2018) and its publisher DOI. The audit explicitly records this conditional interpretation.

Why the leaderboard row 0.2778 is not explained by H33 yet

The saved one-time public-board snapshot records extradr19 at rank 13 with 0.2778; it does not map that participant score to a filename, hash, or organizer receipt. The GEMSDOE32 owner page labels the H33-2-B2 TIFF unscored. Accordingly, the claim that this TIFF earned 0.2778 is contested; the evidence does not establish why the participant received that score.

Conditional metric reasoning, not attribution: the organizer's DTI uses weighted true credit T, false-positive mass F, hidden-truth size K, and weights α=0.2, β=0.8: 
DTI = T / (T + 0.2 F + 0.8 (K − T)) = T / (0.2 (T + F) + 0.8 K)
If the contested file/score pairing were correct, reducing low-credit emissions while retaining high-credit predictions could raise precision and improve DTI. But the H33-specific pruning story is not verified: the prior mass comparison assumes a disputed score-to-file mapping, and the local masking evidence indicates known-catalogue pixels are excluded before both score sums, so deleting only masked pixels would not itself improve the score. A file-level score receipt and confirmation of evaluation-mask semantics are needed before making a causal claim.

Also, 0.3195 was rank 7 in the saved public snapshot, not the top score. The snapshot's rank 1 was 0.3774 (participant name not retained). A model-based calculation that 0.3195 would require 102.7% weighted recall at one assumed waste ratio is algebra only—not a forecast of the private leaderboard and not a reason to optimize to an unauthenticated mapping. Instructions for a future candidate that has passed validation

These steps do not authorize submission of the research TIFF above. Only follow them for a future artifact that has passed the preregistered spatial holdout, controls, positive lower-bound review, exact-byte format audit, and bounded uniqueness comparison.

  1. Confirm the current rules and deadline. Open the official competition description and your authorized participant dashboard. The description says one submission is selected for scoring across the initial and final prize rounds. Do not infer that this means unlimited uploads; verify current allowance, eligible entry and deadline in the portal.
  2. Use the exact promoted file. Download its named .tif artifact from the project site. Do not rename another prediction or use the research-only TIFF linked above.
  3. Validate the exact bytes locally. Check one band, float32, EPSG:32611, 100 m, exact sample transform/bounds, [0,1] range on the authorized footprint, the required NaN-outside convention, nodata metadata, and the SHA-256. Derive the explicit binary footprint from the exact authorized sample template; do not substitute a feature-derived footprint unless the organizer confirms it is identical. Keep the validator report beside the file. A local pass is not an acceptance guarantee.
  4. Join the competition and open the official submission page. Follow any instructions displayed there; upload the GeoTIFF itself, not a ZIP. This project does not log in or submit for you.
  5. Give it an accurate unique name and note. State the model, spacing, emitted-pixel count, validation status, and any relevant limitation. Never claim a conformal guarantee or score that was not established. For the present research artifact, the reference note above is descriptive only and explicitly says research-only.
  6. Keep an audit receipt. Save the exact file hash, portal submission ID, timestamp, displayed score, and status together. Do not infer a score-to-file mapping from participant-level leaderboard rows alone.
  7. If an error appears, preserve it verbatim. Save the complete message and exact submitted file hash. The rejected historical TIFF and organizer-side diagnostic are not available, so the cause of the reported range error remains unknown. A local format pass against either candidate footprint does not prove the portal will accept a file.

Current limitations and unresolved items

  • No candidate in these experiments was scored by the organizer in this session; locked-test metrics are local scores against an incomplete public catalogue, not private labels.
  • Thirteen historical score/file records are not thirteen independent, authoritative observations: twelve are owner-reported pairings and H33-2-B2/0.2778 is explicitly contested; several predictions are nested variants of related fields.
  • The new TIF's uniqueness conclusion covers only 334 historical exact-grid blobs in 55 visible sibling repositories, with inventory-date scope; private, deleted, unindexed, or later artifacts may exist. It proves neither geological novelty nor performance.
  • The NaN-outside TIFF passes only against an explicit mask derived from finite cells in the mirrored sample template. The mirrored training-features-derived footprint disagrees: it has 1,540 feature-only pixels and marks 3,061 sample/label-footprint pixels invalid. The official evaluation footprint and historical range-error cause remain unresolved; local validation does not establish organizer acceptance.
  • Several geological hypotheses need additional work; see the ranked hypothesis review. External sources and known access limits are listed in verified sources.
