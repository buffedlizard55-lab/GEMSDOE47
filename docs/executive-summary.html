IMPORTANT RETRACTION · OLD H60 SCORES ARE NOT FULL-DOMAIN RESULTS

The prior H60 screen used each method's restricted emission domain as the DTI metric-valid mask. Truth cells outside that domain were omitted, including false negatives. Its pooled helper also summed total truth count where the pooled DTI denominator requires false-negative weight. Therefore the old H60/H62 scores and the quoted 0.0989005 floor are historical artifacts, not full evaluated-map or official-metric assurance. New corrected screens preserve the original files and explicitly score all evaluated pixels.
