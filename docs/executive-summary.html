Step-by-Step Submission Guide

Step 1: Download the submission file

Download H51 GeoTIFF (all-finite variant)

Download NaN-outside variant (use if the all-finite variant is rejected)

Step 2: Go to the submission page

Navigate to https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/submit/ (you must be logged in to DrivenData).

Step 3: Upload the file

Click "Choose File" and select the downloaded gems47-h51-multiscale-s2p8-20261007-allfinite.tif file.

Step 4: Add a note

In the "Note (optional)" field, paste:

h51 multiscale-slope-anomaly d2p8 conformal90

This note helps identify the submission later.

Step 5: Submit

Click "Submit" to submit your prediction.

About the Two Rounds

This competition has two scoring rounds:

  1. Initial Prize Round ($50,000): Submissions scored against a fixed private set of new faults labeled by experts before the competition. Top 5 win $10,000 each.
  2. Final Prize Round ($250,000): The same submissions are re-scored against an expanded label set. An expert panel reviews ALL submissions to identify candidate new faults. Predictions that help experts find new faults score higher here. Top 5 win $100K/$70K/$40K/$25K/$15K.

Key insight: In the Final Round, your predictions that experts confirm as real faults become part of the ground truth. This means finding genuinely new, physically plausible faults is more valuable than just matching existing labels.
