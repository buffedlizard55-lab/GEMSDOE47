Hypothesis H60 · round 6 · preregistered

Emit on the lidar
scarp evidence itself.

Every previous family field ranked a proxy of the scarp — the 100 m slope band, corroborated ridges, curvature cascades. H60 ranks the scarp directly: the per-cell maximum of the ranks of six scarp channels of the owner-derived 1 m lidar stack, masked against roads and mine claims, emitted as unit dots at 200 m. On the frozen 41-block holdout it beats the H50 anchor by +73 % on the primary lidar-peak instrument and by +59 % on the independent SGMC off-catalogue fault population — an instrument it does not read.

Step 0 · preregistration

The slate was frozen before any score existed.

Five hypotheses (H60 lidar-crest field, H61 per-trace budget reallocation, H62 additive mixture, H63 catalogue-adjacency gate, H64 instrument refinement), the field definitions, the noise-mask radii, the frozen 41-block design, the instrument set, the controls and the six-condition promotion gate were written and committed (commit 5ea987b) before the first score was computed: h60-hypotheses-preregistered.md. Two implementation deviations are recorded rather than hidden: the per-trace min-1 allocation floor was replaced by deterministic largest-remainder allocation (the floor over-emitted when trace components outnumbered the budget), and the lidar-domain arms cap emission at domain capacity at small spacings, with the actual mass recorded in every row (17,889 of 21,198 dots on the selection half at 2.0 px — H60 outperforms with 84 % of the mass).

Step 1 · the field

The direct detector, not the proxy.

Layers. The owner-derived 1 m lidar scarp stack (built by the family's CI from USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM tiles on the competition's own tile list — 706 of 716 tiles, work resolution 2 m; pinned in registry/data_manifest.json; not organiser-supplied): channels step_max (10 m-scale minus 50 m-scale slope), lappos_max/lapneg_max (LoG crest convexity / base concavity of the 50 m band-passed surface), upface_max/downface_max (band-passed step gradient with/against the regional slope) and cross_max (across-slope). Noise masks: cells within 250 m of a US Census TIGER road or 150 m of a BLM closed mining claim are excluded — the two loudest non-tectonic step sources named in the stack's own caveats. The emission domain is 1,610,706 cells (31.6 % of the evaluated domain).

Why it should catch a catalogue-missing fault. The catalogue is a compilation of mapped traces; a vegetation-obscured or basin-interior Quaternary scarp is missing precisely because nothing except metre-scale topography reveals it. The stack measures that topography directly, so its ranking is blind to mapping status. The road/claim masks remove the step sources that are not tectonic.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

Stated plainly.

The primary lidar-peak instrument is derived from the same owner-built stack the field reads, so primary-instrument numbers are optimistic by construction; the SGMC gain and the 13-artifact rank correlations are the independent evidence, and they are correlations over owner-reported scores, not organiser receipts. The conformal floor is conditional on unverified block exchangeability and covers one future block's proxy DTI — not the private leaderboard. The restored rasters are hash-pinned owner mirrors, not organiser-authenticated bytes. No leaderboard score is claimed for this TIFF, and no submission slot was spent to obtain any number on this page.
