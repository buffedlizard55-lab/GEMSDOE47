# Round-2 candidate hypotheses — ranked before new scoring

**Prepared:** 2026-10-06 UTC, after rereading the standing charter and auditing the current checkout and selected GEMSDOE40–46 methods. **Scoring/code status at registration:** no H47-QC score or raster had been produced. Rankings are ordinal research judgments, not numerical forecasts.

## Scope and novelty boundary

The target is fault geometry, not simply a geothermal prospect. The inspected current code already includes cross-acquisition edge agreement (H47-A), cross-scale magnetic-edge persistence (H47-B), a radiometric-ratio/LoG halo screen (H1), and a cross-fitted geodetic-strain × radiometric-alteration × warm-spring family (H47-GSA). `src/gems47/features.py` uses GDR `thermalclass` proximity, but does not use the three mirror-exported geothermometer-temperature columns. The checkout also contains a broad, unexecuted H47-F proposal for time-persistent thermal/chemistry residuals; the available CSV has no observation-date field, so that temporal hypothesis cannot be tested from this extract.

Sibling review found major overlap for generic magnetic/gravity Euler or basin-margin methods (GEMSDOE40/41/44), fault tips and transverse strands (GEMSDOE45), raw 1 m LiDAR scarp fusion, radiometric composition (GEMSDOE43 and GEMSDOE46-R11), focal mechanisms (GEMSDOE40), and external InSAR / groundwater-head proposals (GEMSDOE46). Those broad ideas are not presented here as fresh suite-wide discoveries. The exact H47-QC operator below was not found in the inspected implementation/results; its thermal/geochemistry *family* is not new, and it refines rather than erases the prior H47-F proposal. Novelty is limited to the reviewed code and records, not all private submissions or worldwide literature.

### Prior-overlap exclusions

Generic joint magnetic/gravity Euler-depth, basin-margin, magnetic persistence, fault-tip/strand completion, focal mechanisms, 1 m LiDAR scarp fusion, radiometric ratios, and ASTER alteration are excluded from this round's “new” slate because inspected sibling repositories already implemented them, or documented them as a close prior candidate. They can remain valuable science; they are not fresh here.

## Public-data feasibility audit (before model code)

- The local mirror CSV contains **27,092 rows**. A source-only grouping by normalized station name and 100 m cell produced 12,975 location groups; 563 groups had at least two plausible (0–300 °C) geothermometer fields plus a plausible measured outlet temperature. Applying the preregistered H47-QC screen (median estimate ≥80 °C; estimate range ≤30 °C) leaves **90 location groups**, with median reservoir estimate 134.7 °C and median measured outlet 35.5 °C. These are *groups*, not 90 independently sampled or time-persistent systems. Only 10 of the 16 fixed spatial blocks contain a qualifying group; coverage is uneven. This is a feasibility count, not a DTI result.
- The GDR CSV also includes `dist_known_fault_px`, a label-derived field. **H47-QC must never read or use it.** The candidate builder must not read labels; labels are for the blocked evaluation only.
- The GDR page describes the public “Well and Spring Temperature and Chemistry” geodatabase and CC BY 4.0. The page does not by itself authenticate the mirrored CSV's column definitions; the local field labels and export are recorded as mirror-derived. No observation dates are available in this CSV, so no temporal persistence claim is allowed.
- For H47-GD, the official ScienceBase item lists a 78.05 MB public ZIP (USGS DOI 10.5066/P14B8XOH), but it was not downloaded or schema/coverage-audited; it is not cleared for a run.
- For H47-HC, USGS's modern API documentation is public and confirms that manual groundwater levels now use `/field-measurements`; the retired `/gwlevels` route must not be used. Exact footprint coverage and compatible aquifer/datum support are still unverified.
- For H47-INS, the NASA ASF/OPERA catalog describes a free 30 m North American DISP-S1 product; an exact full-footprint, repeat-acquisition, quality-mask check has not been completed.

## Source and interpretation limits

- [GDR 1391 — INGENIOUS Great Basin Regional Dataset Compilation](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391), DOI [10.15121/1881483](https://doi.org/10.15121/1881483): public record lists wells/springs, temperature and aqueous chemistry; CC BY 4.0.
- [USGS blind Southern Gabbs Valley geothermal system](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70201803): supports the regional plausibility of concealed systems identified through combined geologic, geochemical, and geophysical evidence; it does not validate H47-QC.
- [USGS geothermometer-calculation guidance](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70033407): warns that thermometer applicability depends on water chemistry and reliable chemical analyses. Agreement among formulas is not independent confirmation if they share a sample, and mixing/dilution can bias temperatures.
- [USGS 2025 geothermometer-depth release](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/66b15b96d34e5d7d928b117c), DOI [10.5066/P14B8XOH](https://doi.org/10.5066/P14B8XOH); [USGS Water Data APIs](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/); [NASA OPERA DISP-S1](https://doi.org/10.5067/SNWG/OPL3DISPS1-V1).
