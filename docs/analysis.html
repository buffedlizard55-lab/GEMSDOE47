Conformal: calculation is not a guarantee

Six calibration blocks yield a nominal order-statistic level of 6/7 ≈ 85.7% only if calibration and future block scores are exchangeable. That assumption is unverified under spatial dependence, and two calibration blocks have no catalogue truth. The computed, clipped lower bound was 0.0. It is not a positive performance floor for private labels or a leaderboard score.
