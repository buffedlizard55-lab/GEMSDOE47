The exact mechanism

D = T / (0.2 (T + F) + 0.8 K)
ΔD > 0 ⇔ ΔT (1 − 0.2 D) − 0.2 D ΔF > 0
F = S − Φ;  T need not equal Φ

Removing predictions can improve D if avoided false-positive cost exceeds lost max-kernel truth credit. Duplicated coverage and low-credit rungs/flanks are plausible pruning targets, not proof that all adjacent terrain is empty. A dot can credit several truth pixels; separated prediction dots alone do not justify T=Φ or D=T/(.2N+.8K).

How large a real improvement is required?

For the explicitly hypothetical case of fixed F and K, reaching 0.3195 from 0.2778 needs about 16.0% more weighted true credit; reaching 0.3774 needs 38.8% more. These are algebraic scenarios, not forecasts; T, F and K on hidden labels are not identified here.

Why λ shrinking and history inversion do not create a free win

D(λp) = λT / (0.2 λ(T+F) + 0.8 K)
1/D(λp) = 0.2(1+F/T) + 0.8 K/(λT)

For positive truth and credit, reducing confidence λ lowers D. Even exact scaling measurements identify ratios, not a unique hidden map or absolute truth mass. Nested/adaptive, rounded scores with unverified file mapping cannot supply conformal coverage. The former λ-probe recommendation is withdrawn. Three historical λ-scaling score observations do not verify an upload/slot count; current quota and slot accounting are unknown from the public official pages checked 2026-10-06. No diagnostic cost is inferred or called free.

What the new test teaches

C1 beats random on public catalogue cores but not the ordinary-terrain pooled control; it also loses to random on the distant SGMC diagnostic. The result does not warrant declaring all scarp methods falsified, nor does it support private transfer. Higher-resolution, properly licensed morphology is a next research direction only after byte/coverage feasibility and a genuinely fresh validation design.
