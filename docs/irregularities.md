## Safe interpretation of the range error

The official task page defines a single-band GeoTIFF contract with grid/range requirements. The reported rejection alone does not identify whether the cause was an out-of-range value, nodata sentinel, data type, transform, or evaluator treatment. Do not claim a root cause without the rejected file and current organizer instructions. For any future output, reopen the exact bytes, validate every in-footprint value and metadata field against the official template, and retain the receipt.
