## Safe interpretation of the range error

The official task page defines a single-band GeoTIFF contract with grid/range requirements. The reported rejection alone does not identify whether the cause was an out-of-range value, nodata sentinel, data type, transform, or evaluator treatment. Do not claim a root cause without the rejected file and current organizer instructions. The H47-B single-scale TIFF's strict local pass uses the mirrored sample-template mask; it fails with the feature-derived mask. The measured footprint discrepancy and authoritative-mask uncertainty are recorded in IR-23 and the [H47-B audit](h47b-mask-audit-20261006.html). For any future output, reopen the exact bytes, validate every in-footprint value and metadata field against the authorized template, and retain the receipt.
