## Later-session attribution, model scope, and open conditions

- **LATI scope:** the twelve score/raster associations used by the later exploratory fit are owner-reported; their raster bytes are hash-pinned, but no organizer receipts authenticate the score-to-file links. LATI is an exploratory inverse under those assumptions, not a private-label map or validated leaderboard-return model.
- **H33 scope:** H33-2-B2 is a separate hash-pinned reference raster, not an authenticated thirteenth score/raster pair. Any fit that assigns it DTI 0.2778 is explicitly conditional on an unverified association. The H47-SAF sensitivity grid is positive at assumed DTI 0.2200 and negative by 0.2400; report only a tested-grid bracket, not an exact root, causal result, or verdict.
- **H47-GSA:** the saved observation-level cross-fit deltas (−0.0611, −0.0450) are negative and conditional on the H33 scenario. They are not a spatial holdout or private-target validation. H47-GSA is not promoted.
- **Model-derived quantities:** K estimates, feature rankings, permutation-null results, and local F1–F5 comparisons describe fits to owner-reported associations and specified reference rasters. They do not measure hidden-label mass, authenticate score mappings, prove the instrument/model valid, or establish causal geological gains. The owner's d2.8 raster is a reference input, not the established spatially blocked holdout best. Known USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are excluded under the official staff clarification; do not infer a gain from deleting masked pixels or a surrounding neighborhood.
- **H47-B input condition:** its recorded screen used public group-hosted mirrors, not authenticated organizer downloads. Hash pins establish byte consistency with those mirrors, not organizer provenance. The current restore manifest has 26 entries; the committed receipt is a historical 23-entry receipt. Before any future H47-B rerun, reconcile the exact input files, hashes, manifest scope, restore record, and provenance against the frozen report. No H47-B rerun is authorized by this review.

## Safe interpretation of the range error

The official task page specifies EPSG:32611, 100 m resolution, training-data bounds, null or NaN outside those bounds, one float32 layer, and values in [0,1]. The reported rejection alone does not identify whether the cause was an out-of-range value, nodata sentinel, data type, transform, or evaluator treatment. A separate scan found that all 12 available sibling `-nan` variants fail a NaN-intolerant range test and all 17 all-finite variants pass, with no finite value outside [0,1] among the 29 files. The all-finite diagnostic writes zeros outside the supplied footprint and does not match the published null/NaN-outside wording; the NaN variant follows an available mirrored sample convention whose provenance and portal acceptance are unverified. These are documented local behaviors, not a root-cause diagnosis of the missing rejected file or proof of acceptance. Do not claim a root cause without the rejected bytes. For any future authorized output, reopen the exact bytes, validate the grid, range, and outside-footprint representation against authenticated organizer inputs, and retain the receipt.
