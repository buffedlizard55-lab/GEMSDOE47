Step 2 · what the round teaches

Four mechanism notes, one refutation.

1. The operating point is a plateau, and 2.0 px sits on it. Every lidar arm emits byte-identical dot sets at 1.6, 1.8 and 2.0 px (verified directly: the top-ranked cells are naturally farther apart than the spacing, so the constraint binds identically), while 1.4 px is strictly worse for every arm and 2.4+ px is worse-or-equal for every lidar arm (H50, the slope anchor, keeps its frozen 2.8 px optimum). The downward extension worked as designed — it bracketed the optimum instead of finding the edge still rising.

2. The mask radius is a real tradeoff, and 250/150 m is its middle. Relaxed masks (H68) win the primary (+2.5%) but lose SGMC (−1.5%); strict masks (H69) lose the primary (−2.8%) but win SGMC (+3.9%, the round's best independent number). Road/claim-adjacent cells hold lidar peaks but not SGMC faults. H60's frozen radii sit at the Pareto middle of the tested ladder.

3. Breadth beats focus on the primary; focus generalizes slightly better. Step-only (H65) wins the step secondary (0.359 vs H60's 0.316) but loses the lappos primary (0.262 vs 0.288); the consensus mean (H66) lands between (0.278 / 0.198). Both beat H60 on SGMC by about 2% (+1.6% and +2.3%). H60's max-of-six aggregation remains the best primary-instrument field tested.

4. H67 is refuted, decisively. The tip-proximity field scores 0.034 on the primary and 0.042 on SGMC — below fixed-seed random on both — and its selection means rise with spacing (wider spacing dilutes a bad field toward random). Catalogue-tip geometry, as formulated, is anti-predictive of off-catalogue scarps. With H63 (whole-trace adjacency) already refuted, no catalogue-geometry field survives in this repository.

H70 instrument study. Downface peaks (+0.350 far) are the weakest lidar channel yet; SGMC variants run from +0.278 (near-catalogue) to −0.355 (>100 m) with the frozen >300 m population at −0.025 — a distance gradient with the opposite sign of the lidar one; the 21 volcanic vents (+0.207, p = 0.52) are uninformative as preregistered. WHAT THIS ROUND IS NOT

Stated plainly.

No new submission was generated: building an artifact from an arm that failed its preregistered gate would be post-hoc gate shopping, and submitting a file that does not beat the holdout best on both instruments would lower, not raise, the probability of winning. The primary-instrument numbers for the lidar arms remain circularity-optimistic (same stack as the field); H67 is exempt and failed anyway. Conformal floors are conditional on unverified block exchangeability and cover one future block's proxy DTI — not the leaderboard.
