## Superseded and historical files

H47-B cross-scale and the H47-SAF artifact are under [`superseded/`](superseded/README.md). Other historical H47-GSA, H47-MAXCOV, H48 and Session 3 TIFFs remain for reproducibility; their hashes, bytes and research-only verdicts are in the [complete artifact register](../all-downloads.html). Do not infer submission eligibility from filename uniqueness, a local raster check, or an archived public proxy score.

Any future promoted artifact requires a distinct new prediction, its own preregistered hypothesis and independent holdout evidence, verified provenance, exact-byte record, appropriate footprint semantics, and a documented review gate. Do not overwrite or relabel any existing file.
