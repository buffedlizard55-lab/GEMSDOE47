Current research order

The prior ranking remains visible to avoid rewriting history. Following H47-B's negative screen, investigate H47-C's official geometry availability and build a defensible current spatial-holdout baseline before detector work. If trace geometry cannot be obtained or its overlap is insufficient, reassess H47-D and H47-F only after their official data coverage is counted. H47-A acquisition-invariant edges are implemented as a screen but lack a valid geological holdout. H47-B should not be retuned on the same locked blocks; any new physical-data version needs a new preregistration and independent holdout.

Slot rule: no hypothesis advances to upload until its genuinely new output beats the current spatially blocked holdout best and relevant controls, with adequate label coverage and a successful file/uniqueness audit. A public-board score or a promising physical story cannot waive this gate.
