1 · H47-C — Native-resolution 3DEP scarp and channel coherence

Layers and operator. USGS 3DEP 1 m bare-earth DEM; native-grid slope, multi-azimuth hillshade, local relief, plan/profile curvature, and cross-profile elevation steps. Derive connected linear scarp candidates at native resolution, then aggregate candidate evidence to the 100 m output grid. Add channel-centerline offsets/knickpoints only if a hydrography layer can be independently sourced and validated.

Physical signature. A repeated linear break in slope or small vertical offset, ideally accompanied by drainage deflection across several channels. Cross-profile geometry and line continuity can reject isolated roughness; explicitly remove roads, canals, terrace margins, and tile seams.

Why it may find missing faults. An unmapped Quaternary trace may have a metre-scale surface rupture absent from the current catalogue. This only targets surface-expressed faults; no scarp does not rule out a blind structure.

Difference from previous work. Prior LiDAR features were pre-derived on a 100 m grid and screened poorly. No inspected candidate detects scarps on 1 m samples before resampling, and this operator does not grow a prediction from catalogue distance or known trace tips.

Source / limitation. USGS 3DEP products and services are free, and the competition page references 1 m tiles. The exact footprint tile list, downloads, vertical datum, and gap mosaic were not validated here. Do not call the layer available until fetched and hashed.

2 · H47-D — ASTER alteration mineralogy × independent structure

Layers and operator. USGS ASTER surface-mineral classes from MRData Open-File Report 2013–1139, emphasizing mapped silicic/argillic and iron-oxide alteration classes. Intersect with an independently derived structural corridor such as a 3DEP lineament or geophysical edge; do not use catalogue distance as the candidate field itself.

Physical signature. Hydrothermal fluids may leave silica/clay/iron alteration along faults. A multi-pixel alteration boundary aligned with independent structure may signal an unmapped conduit. ASTER is a surface-mineral mapper, not a direct subsurface fault detector.

Why it may find missing faults. Alteration may reveal an unregistered trace or extension beyond the mapped geometry. It cannot find buried faults with no exposed alteration and may instead map unrelated lithology or mineralization.

Difference from previous work. H47-GSA used a quantized Th/K ratio and spring/well proximity. This idea uses categorical ASTER mineral products plus an explicit independent-structure interaction, with exposure and lithology controls—not another Th/K threshold or thermal buffer.

Source / limitation. The official USGS page lists class maps and downloadable data, but acquisition and target-footprint intersection were not completed. Verify coordinates, classes, validity masks, coverage, and attribution before implementation.

3 · H47-E — Active-strain / magnetic-edge concordance

Layers and operator. `geod_shearrate`, `geod_dilaterate`, `tmi_hg`, `tmi_vg`, or GeoDAWN `TMI_up150`. Compute directional coherence separately, then score spatial and orientation agreement at a fixed tolerance. Compare against each component alone and randomized alignment controls.

Physical signature. A reactivated fault may localize present deformation along a basement contact. Agreement between independent kinematic and geophysical fields is more specific than an isolated magnetic edge or broad strain hotspot.

Why it may find missing faults. A blind or weakly expressed structure can lack a mapped Quaternary scarp while retaining a deformation and basement-contrast signature. The intersection prioritizes off-catalogue lineaments instead of every magnetic contact.

Difference from previous work. H47-B screened magnetic persistence alone; H47-GSA combined strain with Th/K and thermal points. Neither preregistered this explicit directional strain–magnetic interaction. Previous component screens were negative, so this idea has a lower prior and needs strict ablation/null controls.

Source / limitation. Band names appear in the hash-pinned public mirror; verify them against official metadata. The DrivenData feature page requires participant access and the mirror is not organizer-authenticated. No direct portal raster was obtained.

4 · H47-F — Time-normalized thermal / geochemical station residuals

Layers and operator. Dated well/spring temperature and chemistry records listed through DOE's INGENIOUS GDR compilation, DOI 10.15121/1881483. Only if repeated observations exist, derive station-level robust anomalies after de-duplication and correction for season, elevation, measurement method, and parameter units. Interpolate conservatively; do not make point density the score field.

Physical signature. Sustained warm discharge or coherent fluid chemistry can indicate a permeable fault conduit. Repeated observations may distinguish persistent hydrothermal signal from a one-off measurement or seasonal effect.

Why it may find missing faults. A spring/well anomaly may reveal a conduit along a fault segment not yet mapped as Quaternary geometry. It is supporting evidence, not itself a fault trace; broad geothermal systems may be unrelated to a specific fault.

Difference from previous work. H47-GSA used static proximity to GDR points. H47-F tests repeat-measurement stability and covariate-adjusted station residuals, and requires proof of incremental signal beyond the static baseline.

Source / limitation. GDR is a public compilation, but repeated dates and footprint coverage are unverified. If records are mostly single observations or their geolocation/units are inconsistent, reject this hypothesis before rasterizing.

Validation before any slot

For each unbuilt idea, freeze code, input hashes, blocks, mask semantics, emission mass, and controls before examining holdout results. Require a guarded spatial test, ablation of every feature, random/spatial-permutation/domain controls, fold-level results, and adequate label coverage. Split conformal requires an explicit exchangeability unit and target; the present six-block calibration gives a zero lower bound and has unverified spatial exchangeability. No hypothesis here is a submission recommendation.
