Controls and confounders

Ordinary terrain gradients and raw-band supervision are controls, not novelty. Channels, lithologic contacts, roads, erosional scarps, resampling artifacts and survey boundaries can imitate faults. A low-overlap mask is not proof that any prediction is a geological discovery. Staff explicitly withholds hidden test sources/types/coverage.

The template idea is informed by Sare et al. (2019), which used ≤2 m topography. This four-direction 100 m adaptation is not a replication, a morphologic age estimator or a claim of scientific invention.
