H47-1 · GSA — geodetic strain × hydrothermal alteration × thermal discharge

Layers. geod_shearrate (band 7) and geod_dilaterate (band 8) from training_features.tif; rad_ThK (band 1 of geodawn_extensions_u8.tif, the GeoDAWN thorium/potassium ratio); and proximity to the 27,092 GDR spring-chemistry and water-well points in gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv.

Physical signature targeted. Not an edge and not a curvature transform — a state signature. Four independent indicators of the same process: (1) crustal strain rate localises on structurally weak, actively slipping zones; (2) hydrothermal fluid flow destroys K-feldspar and Th-bearing accessories and precipitates silica and clay, so altered fault rock reads low Th/K — the fitted coefficient is −5.74, i.e. the truth prefers low Th/K, which is the alteration direction and not a generic “high radiometric contrast” direction; (3) dilatation rate marks volume change at releasing bends and stepovers; (4) warm and hot spring discharge is the surface expression of a permeable fault conduit.

Why it catches a fault MISSING from the catalogue. The given labels are the USGS Quaternary fault compilation plus INGENIOUS — both are surface-rupture compilations, mapped from geomorphology. A fault that is actively straining and hydrothermally altered but has no geomorphic expression (buried under basin fill, or blind beneath a clay cap) is invisible to topographic ridge detection and therefore absent from those compilations, yet it is precisely the fault a geothermal resource assessment wants. Strain rate, alteration chemistry and fluid discharge all see it; a DEM does not.

How it differs from prior work. The sibling collection used radiometrics and thermal points only as corroboration gates on a topographic ridge field — a vote that could only remove dots. This is the first time they are the primary belief field, and the first time the sign of the radiometric term is fitted rather than assumed. LATI fits low Th/K and low tmi_vg, which is the opposite of the “high geophysical contrast” prior every sibling repository used.

Result. In the exploratory owner-score-mapped LATI screen, in-fold LOO improved +86.4 % (0.014401 → 0.001960) over four forward-selection steps, K = 15,638. Under a pre-specified geological pool with selection inside each fold, the out-of-fold paired deltas were −0.0611 and −0.0450; score/file mappings are unauthenticated. The in-fold advantage is consistent with optimizer's curse and pool-selection bias. Research only; not promoted; do not upload or spend a slot.

H47-2 · SAF — strike-aligned catalogue-flank continuation

Layers. The catalogue mask itself. A structure tensor (σ = 3 px) gives the local strike; distance_transform_edt(return_indices=True) gives, for every pixel, the offset vector to the nearest catalogue pixel; their dot product decomposes the 0–300 m halo into along-strike continuation, across-strike splay, trace-tip extension (weighted by exp(−d_tip/6) over 47,431 detected endpoints) and bend splay (weighted by 1 − coherence). Implementation: src/gems47/hypotheses.py.

Physical signature targeted. Displacement transfer. Normal faults in the Basin and Range grow by linkage of en-echelon segments, so unmapped geometry concentrates at propagation tips (displacement tapering into a monocline or relay ramp) and at releasing-bend stepovers. Both are directional, not buffer-shaped.

Why it catches a fault MISSING from the catalogue. It is the most literal reading of the organiser’s own words — “newly mapped geometry of an existing fault system”. A trace that stops on the map but continues in the ground produces new-fault pixels immediately along-strike of the mapped endpoint, and those pixels are by definition not in USGS/INGENIOUS.

How it differs from prior work. Directional rather than isotropic continuation. A sibling owner described one 37,654-pixel arm as deleting dots within 2 px of the catalogue, but its page marks H33-2-B2 UNSCORED; the public 0.2778 participant row has no authenticated mapping to that file. Sibling arms h32-1-prethin-tip-euler (owner-reported 0.2649) and h33d-analog-tip-stepover-r30 (owner-reported 0.2632) grew tips but reportedly scored below their own baselines. These associations are exploratory only.

Result — attribution-sensitive, not conclusively falsified. On twelve owner-reported flank-agnostic observations, the halo layers had the strongest in-sample association in that 72-layer screen (dcat_band_0_1.5: LOO gain +55.7 %, β = +6.18; fitted K put 67.1 % within 150 m, a zone reported as 3.45 % of evaluated area). The stored permutation analysis is a useful conditional diagnostic, not an independent truth test. Adding a thirteenth value *assuming* the 0.2778 participant score belongs to the flank-pruned H33-2-B2 raster gives β = +1.42, LOO gain −64.0 %, and a conditional break-even of 0.2200. But that assumption conflicts with the owner's UNSCORED label and has no organizer receipt; the 13-value “falsification” is therefore not validated. Moreover, if known-catalogue pixels are zeroed before both DTI sums, deleting only those pixels cannot itself improve the score. The directional decomposition was also inconclusive in the stored fit: along-strike, across-strike, tip-extension and bend-splay were statistically tied (LOO 0.006568–0.006657) with the plain graded halo (0.006594). Not shipped.

H47-3 · SGMC transfer — catalogue-difference emission

Layers. derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif: 83,593 px of USGS State Geologic Map Compilation fault traces rasterised to the 100 m grid, of which 62,122 px lie more than 300 m from the competition’s given catalogue.

Physical signature targeted. None — this is a set difference, not a detector. It marks SGMC traces absent from the supplied catalogue. Such a line may be mapped and real without being recent, active, newly discovered, or part of the private target.

Why it could overlap off-catalogue targets. It imports mapped traces from an external compilation rather than inferring unobserved faults. Its catalogue difference is a research feature, not organizer-confirmed target truth.

How it differs from prior work. A sibling repository specified this as H60-2 and built sgmc-off-catalogue-44k, but that file was reportedly unscored. The fit below is an exploratory analysis using owner-reported score/file associations, not independent leaderboard validation.

Result — not supported in that exploratory fit. Adding SGMC proximity to the incumbent + flank model worsened reported LOO (0.008124 → 0.009279), and the fitted truth placed 0.5 % of K on SGMC off-catalogue pixels. A frame built from SGMC itself has a tautological self-score; the reported r13-lattice comparison is likewise conditional on its unauthenticated mapping. These results do not establish private-target failure or a general falsification. Not promoted.

H47-4 · DMC — deep-source magnetic continuity ALREADY TESTED — FAILED

Layers. TMI_up150 (band 4 of geodawn_extensions_u8.tif, total magnetic intensity upward-continued 150 m) against the supplied 100 m tmi_hg, tmi_vg, rtp and tc bands.

Physical signature targeted. Depth discrimination by continuation contrast. Upward continuation suppresses shallow sources; a lineament that persists at 150 m of continuation is basement-scale, while one that vanishes is near-surface. Faults through alluvium-filled basins are buried: no scarp, no topographic ridge, but a deep magnetic-offset signature that survives continuation. The discriminator is the ratio of continued to uncontinued edge response, not either alone.

Why it catches a fault MISSING from the catalogue. The Quaternary compilation is geomorphology-driven, so a fault with zero surface expression under basin fill is systematically absent from it. LATI independently fits high tmi_hg (β = +8.55) with low tmi_vg (β = −9.63) — shallow horizontal-gradient contrast without the deep vertical-gradient expression — which is exactly the buried-structure signature, arrived at from the leaderboard rather than from the physics.

How it differs from prior work. Sibling arms used tmi, rtp, tc and tilt-angle zero crossings as edge detectors at a single scale. None used upward continuation as a depth filter, and none combined it with the vertical-gradient band as a contrast pair. H61-3 (tilt-angle zero crossings) is the nearest relative and is a single-scale edge detector.

This hypothesis is not new, and it has already been tested and rejected. While merging the two GEMSDOE47 sessions it emerged that the earlier session built exactly this idea as H47-B — cross-scale persistence of TMI_up150 magnetic edges — and ran it through a preregistered gate with a locked test block and nontrivial controls. Result (validation-h47b-20261006.md, screen report):

Recorded here so the next session does not pay to learn it twice.

H47-5 · λ-probe — offline metric algebra NO SLOT AUTHORIZED

Layers. None. This is a measurement idea, not a geological candidate.

Conditional identity. Under a fixed target, mask, footprint, and the displayed local DTI formula, positive scaling gives T(λp)=λT(p) and F(λp)=λF(p), so 1/DTI(λ) is linear in 1/λ. Exact returns could recover terms only if the organizer uses the same scoring and scaling semantics.

Limits and decision. The private target and exact portal behavior are unavailable here. It is not established that a null-add has zero marginal credit, cannot lower rank, or reveals the intended quantities. This algebra is retained for offline reasoning only; score probes do not beat the spatially blocked holdout and are not authorized in any competition slot, including an unlimited round. No submission experiment was run or recommended.

Other historical screens (exploratory)

Earlier owner-reported fits ranked 72 layers on 12 observations and 65 on 13. The score-to-file associations are not organizer-authenticated, so the results below are sensitivity analyses—not independent private-target validation or proof that a data family is exhausted.

  • LiDAR scarp morphology (12 sibling-derived 100 m layers; claimed source-tile coverage and dates were not verified): lidar_upface_max ranked 21st of 72 on 12 observations and did not enter the 13-observation forward selection. lidar_coh100, lidar_strike and lidar_valid ranked poorly in that fit. Exact USGS 3DEP 1 m coverage for the competition template remains unverified; no causal explanation is inferred.
  • Thermal-spring temperature class: in that owner-reported fit, thermal_hot_prox ranked poorly while thermal_warm_prox entered the selection. This does not establish general effects for temperature classes or discharge density.
  • Volcanic vent proximity: the restored mirror lists 21 vents in the footprint; this small count is a coverage constraint, not evidence that vents cannot carry signal.
  • Earthquake density (ieq_n100a15, deq_n100a15): +47.1 % and +26.6 % on 13 observations, but neither entered the forward selection ahead of strain rate.
  • Gradients of selected supplied bands (cond_surf_grad, depth_to_base_surf_grad, iso_grav_anom_grad): owner-reported LOO fits were poor (−89.7 %, −72.7 %, −64.4 %). This does not exhaust all gradient methods or certify private-target failure.
