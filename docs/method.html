Evidence boundary. No organizer receipt links the historical participant rows to exact TIFF hashes. LATI uses owner-reported score/file associations, so its fitted q, K, LOO rankings, and apparent controls are exploratory proxies—not verified hidden truth, leaderboard performance, or private-target validation. The H47-QC screen elsewhere in this repository is a separate public-catalogue test and also is not private-set validation.

1 · The problem with the previous proxy

An earlier sibling analysis represented hidden truth as π ~ exp(−d(H19-5)/1.85 px), a scatter around the group's own field. Any rule tuned to cover that field better will win against that constructed model by design. Its owner-reported evidence says greedy_smoothfield beat the incumbent by +0.0247 ± 0.0005 with 12/12 draws positive. This illustrates model dependence; it is not independent evidence about private labels.

LATI instead fits equations to twelve historical participant scores and recovered rasters. The local bytes can be SHA-256 pinned, but the participant-score-to-file associations are not authenticated by organizer receipts. The resulting scores are therefore provisional inputs, not confirmed measurements of the hidden label set. They support sensitivity analysis, not leaderboard claims.

2 · What thirteen provisional scalars can constrain

Write G for the hidden new-fault pixel set, K = |G|, and q(x) = P(x ∈ G) for its intensity. For a submission p with dot set D(p):

T(p)  = Σ_{g∈G} max_{x∈D(p)} k(d(x,g))  =  Σ_x q(x)·w_p(x),   w_p(x) = max_{y∈D(p)} k(d(x,y))    (L1)
Φ(p)  = Σ_{x∈D(p)} E_q[κ(x)],            κ(x) = max_{g∈G} k(d(x,g))
      = Σ_x q(x)·a_p(x),                 a_p(x) = Σ_{y∈D(p)} k(d(x,y))                            (L2)
F(p)  = S(p) − Φ(p)
DTI   = T / (α·(T + F) + β·K)              α = 0.2, β = 0.8, k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0)

(L1) is exact. w_p depends only on p, so T is a linear functional of the unknown q. (L2) is first-order and is used only for the 13-observation screening fits; the emission path uses the exact Bernoulli expectation instead (§5).

Under the displayed formula, each reported DTI gives one nonlinear equation in functionals of the proxy field q. The twelve-owner-reported observation set includes different raster shapes and masses (37,654 to 343,816 pixels), which can constrain an inverse model. This resembles tomography, but identifiability depends on the raster/score mappings, mask, functional approximation and model class; it is not a reconstruction of authenticated hidden labels.

3 · Local formula reductions, verified to machine precision

FN_w = K − T          (the two published sums are term-wise complements)
FP_w = S − Φ          (k is decreasing, so max_g k(d(x,g)) is the kernel at the NEAREST truth pixel)

DTI   = T / (α·(T + S − Φ) + β·K)                        ← the design equation
1/DTI = α + α·(F/T) + β·(K/T)
DTI(λp) = λT / (λα(T+F) + βK)   ⇒   1/DTI is LINEAR in 1/λ  ← the λ-probe identity
d(DTI) > 0  ⇔  dT·(1/DTI − α) > α·dF                   ← the marginal rule
for dT = k, dF = 1 − k this reduces to   k > α·DTI       ← a dot pays iff k > 0.0520 at DTI 0.26

These identities are asserted in tests/test_metric.py against a literal O(N²) transcription of the published formulas and the organizer's worked example (TPw 3.00, FPw 1.89, FNw 2.00 → 0.60). This verifies the local algebra implementation; it does not authenticate the organizer's production scorer or prove that owner-reported rows used it.

4 · The proxy model and its parametrisation

The first proxy attempt used q(x) = expit(β₀ + Σβₘuₘ(x)). It was numerically unstable: coefficients reached ±2,728 and a three-equation solve on the nested d2.8 ⊂ d1.5 ⊂ h19-5 family had condition number 3,136. This documents failure of that local parametrisation, not a property of the true hidden labels.

A later proxy parameterization used a softmax with an explicit mass parameter:

q(x) = K · exp(Σₘ βₘ·uₘ(x)) / Σ_x exp(Σₘ βₘ·uₘ(x))
  • In this proxy, the diffuse r13-lattice and placeholder probes have 300 m halos that average the footprint, so their owner-reported score associations provide a relatively shape-insensitive historical estimate of K; this is not a verified truth count;
  • the βₘ are estimated from the concentrated probes under this proxy model;
  • β is shift-invariant, so no intercept has to be searched;
  • it cannot degenerate into a runaway hard threshold while still reporting a plausible K.

Every layer is reduced to a rank percentile (uint8, 0–255) over the evaluated pixel set first, which makes the fit scale-free and immune to the float32-min nodata sentinel −3.4028234663852886e+38 — a value np.isfinite does not catch. All 19 supplied bands contain it.

The binned fast path

Because T, Φ and K are all linear in q, and q depends on x only through the selected uₘ(x), all three are exactly determined by the joint histogram of the quantised selected features together with the per-cell sums of w_p and a_p. Aggregating onto that grid makes a model evaluation 0.015 ms instead of 210 ms — 700× faster — with a measured error of 1.1e-05 in DTI at 256 levels. That is what makes 65-layer screens, forward selection, leave-one-observation-out CV and 300 bootstrap replicates affordable on 2 vCPU.

The permutation null is the important one. A geometrically thin layer like dcat_band_0_1.5 produces a highly concentrated q automatically, and concentration alone raises DTI for concentrated submissions while lowering it for diffuse ones. All fourteen layers beat that null 10/10, so the signal was spatial, not an artefact of thinness.

6 · Cross-fitting — the only test that can rank a NEW candidate

A candidate built by maximising a fitted objective, then scored by that same objective, always wins. Two biases produce that pattern and both are removed here:

  • B1 optimizer’s curse — the candidate is selected on the objective it is then judged by;
  • B2 pool-selection bias — the candidate layers were themselves ranked by LOO on the same thirteen observations, so even the LOO number is optimistic.

The cure: fix the layer pool a priori from geology (twelve layers implied by “faults indicative of geothermal resources”: three geodetic strain-rate bands, two radiometric ratios, two thermal-discharge proximities, two seismicity densities, two magnetic bands, surface conductivity) — never from the data; run forward selection inside each fold; build the candidate from fold A’s model and score it with fold B’s, and vice versa; score the incumbent with the same out-of-fold model so the comparison is paired.

7 · Two bugs the review passes caught

7a · Φ was an upper bound, not an estimate — and it drove FPw negative

The first-order Φ ≈ ⟨q, a_p⟩ replaces max by sum. That is valid while c(x) = Σ_y q(y)k(d(x,y)) ≪ 1, and it is an upper bound on the truth, never an estimate: κ(x) = max_g k(d(x,g)) ≤ 1, so Φ ≤ S always, whereas ⟨q, a_p⟩ is unbounded. On a concentrated belief field with a dense emission it exceeded S and the reported FPw went to −47,502, making the predicted DTI 1.6459 — impossible for the real metric. Measured over-estimate: +0.8 % at q ~ 0.002, +7.8 % at q ~ 0.02, +87.4 % at q ~ 0.2.

Replaced everywhere by the exact Bernoulli expectation over kernel rings: E[max] = Σⱼ kⱼ(Qⱼ₋₁ − Qⱼ) with Qⱼ = Π_{rings 1..j} Π_δ (1 − q(x+δ)). A hard assertion now fails the build if FPw ever goes negative.

7b · Stable form and a boundary-fill bug in the local proxy

The ring-product form above subtracts nearly equal products. At q ~ 2e-3 it lost ~2e-4 per pixel to float64 cancellation, which summed over 150,000 dots and again pushed FPw to −28.2. Rewritten as

E[κ] = 1 − Σ_{m=1..6} (k_m − k_{m+1})·Q_m,     k_7 := 0

which only ever subtracts non-negative terms from 1, so E[κ] ∈ [0,1] by construction. Dense and sparse implementations now agree to 1e-10.

Separately, a dense-shift implementation filled out-of-grid positions with 0.0 in a 1 − q array, which spuriously implied q = 1 outside the raster. This was a boundary-handling bug in the local proxy, not evidence about real hidden faults. It inflated E[κ] along the edges and made dense/sparse calculations disagree by 29%. The fill was corrected to 1.0 (q = 0 outside), after which the local paths agreed.

8 · Masking — staff wording and a non-authenticated byte comparison

DrivenData staff said known-catalogue pixels are masked/excluded from evaluation. Locally, the recovered 8GEMSDOE_Hedge-v2_submission.tif and gemsdoe-ens12-adopted-7f00890a.tif are byte-identical off the catalogue; the former adds 60,988 catalogue pixels at p = 1. Their owner-reported participant rows both show 0.1563 to four decimals, but no organizer receipt links either hash to that return.

  • If those file/score associations are correct, the equal rounded values constrain the effect of catalogue-only additions; they do not reveal whether the scorer masks before both sums or uses another implementation with the same displayed result.
  • A local unmasked calculation would treat the added pixels differently, but the participant-level observation is not an authenticated scorer test.

The staff statement and owner-reported comparison are consistent with known-catalogue pixels being excluded from both score sums. They do not establish the portal implementation. The local mask_mode="zero" is one explicit interpretation; "fp_only" is an alternative local diagnostic, not a proven or disproven account of the private scorer.

Holdout implication is conditional. If an evaluator removes known-catalogue pixels from both truth and prediction, a holdout whose only truth is the catalogue has K = 0 and cannot measure candidate performance. This explains why the local catalogue frame is not used for selection; it does not identify the organizer’s exact implementation or validate owner-reported scores.

9 · Honest limits of LATI

  • Thirteen scalars cannot identify 59 coefficients. The reported answer is a ranking of layer families, not a map of G.
  • Six of the thirteen are nested thinnings of one field, so the effective sample size is well below thirteen. The three-equation solve on that nested triple alone had condition number 3,136 and collapsed — a documented dead end.
  • The thirteen DTIs are owner-reported transcriptions of the public board. No organiser receipt exists. They are treated as exact and flagged.
  • K identified here is the public-chunk hidden mass. Whether scoring is restricted to a public-chunk footprint, or runs over the whole raster with only public-chunk truth, is unresolved — and it changes whether off-chunk mass is neutral or pure FP.
  • LATI can tell us the truth is near our best field. It cannot tell us which geology that is. That is why the positive controls are reported at the top of every screen instead of being quietly excluded.
