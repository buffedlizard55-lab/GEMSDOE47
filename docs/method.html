1 · The problem with the previous instrument

A sibling repository’s truth model — the one every downstream decision in that family was scored against — draws hidden truth as π ~ exp(−d(H19-5)/1.85 px): a scatter around the group’s own best field. Any rule that covers that field better then wins by construction. Its own evidence file records exactly that: greedy_smoothfield beat the incumbent by +0.0247 ± 0.0005 with 12/12 draws positive. The model cannot falsify the field it was built from, so the number carries no information.

LATI replaced that circular field with a score-inversion model using owner-reported pairings: exact historical rasters are hash-pinned, and reported DTIs are transcribed from owner materials. Twelve pairings are represented in the registry; the H33-2-B2 / 0.2778 pairing is explicitly contested and is not an organizer receipt. These values may constrain a hidden-label model only under the recorded pairing and score-semantics assumptions; they are not direct or authoritative measurements of private labels.

2 · What thirteen historical scalars can constrain (conditionally)

Write G for the hidden new-fault pixel set, K = |G|, and q(x) = P(x ∈ G) for its intensity. For a submission p with dot set D(p):

T(p)  = Σ_{g∈G} max_{x∈D(p)} k(d(x,g))  =  Σ_x q(x)·w_p(x),   w_p(x) = max_{y∈D(p)} k(d(x,y))    (L1)
Φ(p)  = Σ_{x∈D(p)} E_q[κ(x)],            κ(x) = max_{g∈G} k(d(x,g))
      = Σ_x q(x)·a_p(x),                 a_p(x) = Σ_{y∈D(p)} k(d(x,y))                            (L2)
F(p)  = S(p) − Φ(p)
DTI   = T / (α·(T + F) + β·K)              α = 0.2, β = 0.8, k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0)

(L1) is exact. w_p depends only on p, so T is a linear functional of the unknown q. (L2) is first-order and is used only for the 13-observation screening fits; the emission path uses the exact Bernoulli expectation instead (§5).

Under the assumed score/file pairings and a fixed evaluator mask, each DTI is a nonlinear constraint in functionals of the unknown intensity q. The twelve owner-reported pairs plus one contested record span different raster structures, but the records are few, several are nested, and one score mapping is unresolved. They do not establish unique identification or true tomography; solutions depend on model class, priors, and the reported-score assumptions.

3 · Exact reductions, verified to machine precision

FN_w = K − T          (the two published sums are term-wise complements)
FP_w = S − Φ          (k is decreasing, so max_g k(d(x,g)) is the kernel at the NEAREST truth pixel)

DTI   = T / (α·(T + S − Φ) + β·K)                        ← the design equation
1/DTI = α + α·(F/T) + β·(K/T)
DTI(λp) = λT / (λα(T+F) + βK)   ⇒   1/DTI is LINEAR in 1/λ  ← the λ-probe identity
d(DTI) > 0  ⇔  dT·(1/DTI − α) > α·dF                   ← the marginal rule
for dT = k, dF = 1 − k this reduces to   k > α·DTI       ← a dot pays iff k > 0.0520 at DTI 0.26

All five are asserted in tests/test_metric.py against a literal O(N²) transcription of the four published formulas and against the organiser’s own worked example (TPw 3.00, FPw 1.89, FNw 2.00 → 0.60).

4 · The model, and the parametrisation that made it identifiable

The first attempt used q(x) = expit(β₀ + Σβₘuₘ(x)). It failed: the optimiser ran coefficients to ±2,728, degenerating q into a hard indicator while reporting an implausible K, and a three-equation solve on the nested d2.8 ⊂ d1.5 ⊂ h19-5 family came back with condition number 3,136 and a collapsed solution. K and shape were confounded.

The fix is a softmax with the total mass as an explicit parameter:

q(x) = K · exp(Σₘ βₘ·uₘ(x)) / Σ_x exp(Σₘ βₘ·uₘ(x))
  • Within this model class, K is most strongly constrained by the diffuse probes — r13-lattice and placeholder — whose 300 m halos average the footprint and are less sensitive to shape; this is conditional on owner-reported scores and fixed evaluator semantics;
  • the βₘ are identified by the concentrated probes;
  • β is shift-invariant, so no intercept has to be searched;
  • it cannot degenerate into a runaway hard threshold while still reporting a plausible K.

Every layer is reduced to a rank percentile (uint8, 0–255) over the evaluated pixel set first, which makes the fit scale-free and immune to the float32-min nodata sentinel −3.4028234663852886e+38 — a value np.isfinite does not catch. All 19 supplied bands contain it.

The binned fast path

Because T, Φ and K are all linear in q, and q depends on x only through the selected uₘ(x), all three are exactly determined by the joint histogram of the quantised selected features together with the per-cell sums of w_p and a_p. Aggregating onto that grid makes a model evaluation 0.015 ms instead of 210 ms — 700× faster — with a measured error of 1.1e-05 in DTI at 256 levels. That is what makes 65-layer screens, forward selection, leave-one-observation-out CV and 300 bootstrap replicates affordable on 2 vCPU.

The permutation null is the important one. A geometrically thin layer like dcat_band_0_1.5 produces a highly concentrated q automatically, and concentration alone raises DTI for concentrated submissions while lowering it for diffuse ones. 6 · Cross-fitting — a necessary but insufficient anti-optimism check

A candidate built by maximising a fitted objective and then scored by that same objective is optimistically biased. Cross-fitting reduces that bias within this retrospective model, but it does not validate the owner-reported mappings, recover private labels, or replace a preregistered spatial holdout. Two biases produce that pattern and both are removed here:

  • B1 optimizer’s curse — the candidate is selected on the objective it is then judged by;
  • B2 pool-selection bias — the candidate layers were themselves ranked by LOO on the same thirteen observations, so even the LOO number is optimistic.

The cure: fix the layer pool a priori from geology (twelve layers implied by “faults indicative of geothermal resources”: three geodetic strain-rate bands, two radiometric ratios, two thermal-discharge proximities, two seismicity densities, two magnetic bands, surface conductivity) — never from the data; run forward selection inside each fold; build the candidate from fold A’s model and score it with fold B’s, and vice versa; score the incumbent with the same out-of-fold model so the comparison is paired.

7 · Two bugs the review passes caught

7a · Φ was an upper bound, not an estimate — and it drove FPw negative

The first-order Φ ≈ ⟨q, a_p⟩ replaces max by sum. That is valid while c(x) = Σ_y q(y)k(d(x,y)) ≪ 1, and it is an upper bound on the truth, never an estimate: κ(x) = max_g k(d(x,g)) ≤ 1, so Φ ≤ S always, whereas ⟨q, a_p⟩ is unbounded. On a concentrated belief field with a dense emission it exceeded S and the reported FPw went to −47,502, making the predicted DTI 1.6459 — impossible for the real metric. Measured over-estimate: +0.8 % at q ~ 0.002, +7.8 % at q ~ 0.02, +87.4 % at q ~ 0.2.

Replaced everywhere by the exact Bernoulli expectation over kernel rings: E[max] = Σⱼ kⱼ(Qⱼ₋₁ − Qⱼ) with Qⱼ = Π_{rings 1..j} Π_δ (1 − q(x+δ)). A hard assertion now fails the build if FPw ever goes negative.

7b · The stable form, and a boundary fill that meant “certain truth”

The ring-product form above subtracts nearly equal products. At q ~ 2e-3 it lost ~2e-4 per pixel to float64 cancellation, which summed over 150,000 dots and again pushed FPw to −28.2. Rewritten as

E[κ] = 1 − Σ_{m=1..6} (k_m − k_{m+1})·Q_m,     k_7 := 0

which only ever subtracts non-negative terms from 1, so E[κ] ∈ [0,1] by construction. Dense and sparse implementations now agree to 1e-10.

Separately, the dense shift filled out-of-grid positions with 0.0 — which, applied to 1 − q, means q = 1, i.e. certain hidden truth beyond every border. That inflated E[κ] along all four edges and made the dense and sparse paths disagree by 29 %. The fill is now 1.0 (q = 0 outside), and the two paths agree exactly.

8 · Catalogue masking — evidence and interpretation

DrivenData staff state that known USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue pixels are masked from evaluation. A restored-byte comparison provides owner-reported corroboration: 8GEMSDOE_Hedge-v2_submission.tif and gemsdoe-ens12-adopted-7f00890a.tif are identical off the catalogue; the former adds 54,533 catalogue pixels, while both associated owner records report 0.1563. However, no organizer receipt maps those exact hashes to the reported score, so this is not a definitive test of the private scorer's mask implementation.

The local metric implementation defaults to mask_mode="zero", consistent with the staff statement and the owner-reported comparison; it retains "fp_only" as an alternative for sensitivity checks. Treat the exact operational mask as an assumption until a verified organizer receipt or authoritative evaluator specification confirms it.

Holdout consequence under the local zero-mask assumption. A holdout whose truth is exactly the known catalogue would have g & evaluated empty, K = 0, and DTI ≡ 0. A separate unmasked catalogue frame can be a proxy for coverage but measures a different target. It is not selected on as a score for off-catalogue faults.

9 · Honest limits of LATI

  • Thirteen reported scalars cannot identify 59 coefficients or recover a unique map of G. The model's output is a prior-sensitive ranking of feature families, not a direct truth map.
  • Several predictions are nested and correlated, and the H33-2-B2/0.2778 pairing is contested. The effective sample is far below thirteen independent, authoritative observations.
  • The DTI values are owner-reported transcriptions under recorded file pairings. No organizer receipt authenticates all of them, and they are not a full set of public leaderboard rows.
  • Which spatial footprint the organizer actually scores is unresolved in this checkout. It changes whether mass outside the known public chunk is neutral or false positive.
  • LATI cannot identify which geology is correct or validate a private leaderboard prediction. Its in-fold results are exploratory; the cross-fit deltas were negative and the model did not promote.

10 · Separate split-conformal spacing audit (H47-B)

The frozen H47-B/single-scale spacing audit selects spacing on five blocks, calibrates on six separate blocks and locks five blocks for testing. Both arms select 5 px / 500 m on the selection blocks. Six calibration scores yield rank 6/6 and a nominal one-sided marginal level of 6/7 = 85.7% only under exchangeability. Both clipped lower prediction bounds are 0.000. Two calibration cores contain no catalogue truth, and spatial block exchangeability is unverified. This is not cellwise coverage, private-label coverage, or a leaderboard guarantee. The experiment and assumptions are fully recorded in the audit JSON and the results analysis.
