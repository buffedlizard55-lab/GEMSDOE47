What was tested

H65 independently ranks three owner-derived LiDAR descriptors over the fixed H60 emission domain: localized step amplitude (step_max), negative curvature/base concavity (lapneg_max), and positive curvature/crest convexity (lappos_max). It takes the cube root of the product of the ranks and re-ranks the consensus. The physical hypothesis is that paired step/crest/base morphology may distinguish some fault scarps from isolated channel banks or curvature extrema. Max-pooled channels do not preserve sub-cell ordering; this remains a proxy-producing hypothesis, not fault confirmation.

Emission is limited to the H60 mask: in-footprint, outside the known catalogue, valid owner-derived LiDAR, at least 250 m from owner-rasterized TIGER roads and 150 m from owner-rasterized BLM closed mining claims. The historical LiDAR-peak proxy is reported separately; the primary H65 selection target is the independent public SGMC off-catalogue trace raster (>300 m from the training catalogue). SGMC is not private truth and carries map/contact bias.

Four geological hypotheses (H65–H68), source availability, physical signatures, novelty limits, costs, and the frozen H65 protocol are in the pre-score preregistration. No additional data were required for H65. The underlying LiDAR descriptor stack is an owner-derived mirror of USGS 3DEP inputs, not an organizer-supplied file.
