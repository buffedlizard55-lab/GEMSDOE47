## H65 frozen screen definition

1. **Input provenance:** use only the hash-pinned local competition-grid mirror and H60 LiDAR/road/claim mirrors. Do not imply the mirrors are organizer-authenticated. Do not use the reference H33 raster or any historical submission to construct predictions.
2. **Emission domain:** reuse H60's predeclared domain: supplied footprint, outside the known-fault catalogue, valid LiDAR, at least 250 m from the owner-rasterized TIGER roads and at least 150 m from the owner-rasterized BLM closed claims.
3. **Field:** compute separate domain-only empirical ranks for `step_max`, `lapneg_max`, and `lappos_max`; calculate `cbrt(rank_step * rank_lapneg * rank_lappos)` per cell and re-rank that score over the same domain. Do not tune channels, exponents, masks, or thresholds after seeing any H65 score.
4. **Emission:** greedy maximum-score points, minimum Euclidean separation in `{2.0, 2.4, 2.8, 3.2, 3.6, 4.0, 4.6}` pixels; pixel size is 100 m. Whole-map and block budgets target 37,654 predictions in proportion to evaluated pixels. If a masked block cannot fit its budget, record actual emission; do not silently pretend the budget was met.
5. **Spatial split:** reproduce the frozen H60 contiguous 8×8 guarded blocks and exact 20 selection / 21 calibration roles from `evidence/h60/blocks.json`, including every eligible block and zero-score rows. Guards are 3 pixels (300 m). Do not move blocks or choose roles after scoring.
6. **Primary selection/proxy instrument:** independent SGMC off-catalogue trace raster (`derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8`, >300 m from the training catalogue), reported separately from the same-stack LiDAR-peak instrument. SGMC is a geological-map proxy, not hidden competition truth; its contact/bedrock bias is known.
7. **Spacing selection:** choose the spacing with the highest selection-half mean block DTI; ties choose larger spacing. Apply a one-sided, max-over-settings split-conformal lower prediction band to the **calibration half**, using Lei et al.'s finite-sample order-statistic correction: `k = ceil((n_cal + 1) × 0.90)`. The conformal target is one future exchangeable block's **public SGMC-proxy DTI**, not the mean, pooled map score, private leaderboard score, or Phase 2 label set.
8. **Candidate comparison:** compare the selected H65 operating point against H60 selected by the same SGMC-selection-half rule; compute the paired per-block difference and the same one-sided conformal lower prediction bound. **No submission slot is used.** H65 is not promoted unless the preregistered independent-proxy comparison beats H60 and the paired lower bound is positive; even then a separate confirmation is needed before any portal slot.
9. **Adaptivity disclosure:** H60's frozen spacing screen is the repository's pre-registered calibration/selection result. H65 is a new hypothesis motivated after prior H60 work, and reuses those already-observed coarse spatial blocks. Therefore H65's computed bound is exploratory and **not** a fresh confirmatory guarantee against arbitrary prior model search. Report the mathematical nominal rank/coverage and this limitation; do not relabel it as private-score assurance.
10. **If H65 fails:** do not alter the field and rerun on the same blocks. Publish the negative result, retain H60 as the best already-tested local proxy candidate, and mark any H65 TIFF research-only. A future H65 retry needs a new, frozen validation population and a confirmatory split not used to design the candidate.

## Formal conformal interpretation

For the prior H60 sweep, the selection/calibration block roles and spacing set were frozen before scoring. There are `n_cal = 21` calibration blocks; at nominal 90%, the split-conformal order statistic is `k = ceil(22 × 0.90) = 20`. Thus the finite-sample marginal coverage level is at least `20/22 = 90.91%` **if and only if** the calibration block score vectors and one future block score vector are exchangeable under the chosen public-proxy target. Spatial blocking reduces direct pixel overlap; it does not prove exchangeability. The recorded H60 selected-spacing lower bound is `0.0989005245` DTI on the `lappos_t200_d3` public proxy. It is **not** a floor for the held-out private labels, a pooled-region DTI guarantee, or a Phase 2 guarantee.

Reference: Lei, G'Sell, Rinaldo, Tibshirani & Wasserman (2018), “Distribution-Free Predictive Inference for Regression,” *JASA* 113(523):1094–1111, [DOI 10.1080/01621459.2017.1307116](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2017.1307116).
