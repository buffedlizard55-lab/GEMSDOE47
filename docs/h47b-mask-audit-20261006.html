Format boundary

Local pass depends on the mask

The byte-level read-back check passes only when the explicit mask is derived from finite cells in the mirrored sample_submission.tif. It is a local format pass only—not scientific validation and not organizer acceptance.

The paired all-finite diagnostic has the same positive mask, but zeros outside and no NaN nodata tag. It fails the strict NaN-outside validator. The original portal error's cause remains unknown.

Measured footprint mismatch

The mirrored sample template and labels each have 5,167,373 valid cells; the feature-derived validity mask has 5,165,852. Windowed comparison found 1,540 feature-valid cells outside the sample/label footprint and 3,061 sample/label cells invalid in features. Strict validation fails when the feature-derived mask replaces the explicit sample-template mask. Official evaluation-mask semantics and provenance are unresolved.

See IR-47-021 and IR-23, the machine-readable footprint comparison, and the mask reproduction script. Do not infer the official footprint from this public mirror.
