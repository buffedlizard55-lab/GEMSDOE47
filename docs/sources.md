## Provenance rule

Official organizer pages establish competition requirements; official USGS/DOE/NASA records establish data provenance and stated availability; local results require local data plus reproducible code; owner/site claims remain labeled as claims unless independently matched to an organizer receipt. A URL being listed as downloadable is not evidence that this workspace has downloaded the file.
