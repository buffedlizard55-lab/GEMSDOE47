Metric algebra and what artifact counts do—and do not—show

1/DTI = α + α·(F/T) + β·(K/T)        α = 0.2, β = 0.8
FN_w  = K − T          exactly
FP_w  = S − Φ          exactly,  Φ = Σ_{x∈D} κ(x),  κ(x) = max_{g∈G} k(d(x,g))
DTI   = T / (α·(T + S − Φ) + β·K)

marginal rule:  add a dot  ⇔  dT·(1/DTI − α) > α·dF
                for dT = k, dF = 1 − k this is   k > α·DTI
                at DTI 0.2600 → k > 0.0520      at DTI 0.2778 → k > 0.0556
                k = 1 − d/3 px  ⇒  a dot pays only within ≈ 2.84 px ≈ 284 m of a truth pixel

x = T/K required for a target DTI at waste ratio ρ = F/K:   x = (αρ + β)/(1/DTI − α)

At T/K = 1, the maximum F/K compatible with a target is (1/DTI − α − β)/α: 10.6495 for DTI 0.3195 and 8.2485 for DTI 0.3774. Thus an illustrative F/K = 8.02 makes both targets feasible under this algebra; it does not establish an observed ratio for any score-associated submission. Separately, if f = F/T, then T/K = β/[1/DTI − α(1 + f)]: for target 0.3195, f = 8 gives 60.16 % and f = 4 gives 37.56 %. These are different denominator scenarios. No leaderboard score alone identifies either ratio or authenticates a TIFF mapping; see the dated participant-level snapshot.
