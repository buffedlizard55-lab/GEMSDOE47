H33 0.2778: evidence, not an assumed score receipt

Bottom line: the H33 and H27 TIFF masks have a verified nesting relationship, but no organizer receipt in this repository maps the public 0.2778 observation to the exact H33 TIFF bytes. The pinned GEMSDOE32 owner README calls H33 UNSCORED and describes 0.2747 as a projection. We therefore cannot honestly explain 0.2778 as the measured score of this particular file or claim that its pruning caused a score increase.

1. What is verified about the files

The pinned GEMSDOE32 H33 zero-outside TIFF has 37,654 positive cells (SHA-256 c55bafc470054e8271d1cb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9). It is an exact subset of the pinned GEMSDOE28 H27 parent with 40,199 positive cells (SHA-256 2fc94a38d77f74d4f4ed1a97a83e7bb71a1ceea090515ec641e6681cc47c44c8): 2,545 parent-only cells and no H33-only cells. All 2,545 removed cells are within two 100 m pixels of the given-catalogue mask (1,201 within one pixel and 1,344 in the two-pixel ring). A separate 41,507-positive GEMSDOE27 all-increments raster is not the parent in this comparison.

These are byte- and mask-level facts, not evidence of hidden-label credit or a change in organizer score. The pinned mask audit records the comparison. The pinned GEMSDOE32 README is an owner source, not an organizer receipt; its H33 entry says unscored and gives 0.2747 as a projection.

2. What the metric algebra says

For the published distance-weighted Tversky index, let T be weighted true credit, S the total prediction mass, M the prediction mass weighted by each prediction's best kernel to truth, and |G| the hidden-truth mass. The official formula simplifies to

DTI = T / (0.2 · (T + S − M) + 0.8 · |G|)

Under the narrow marginal case where one added unit of prediction mass earns a new kernel-weighted credit w and becomes the best cover of that truth cell, adding it improves DTI only when w > 0.2 × DTI. At a hypothetical DTI of 0.2778, the break-even credit is 0.05556. This is an algebraic condition, not a measurement of the 2,545 removed cells.

The given-catalogue cells are excluded from scoring; there is no automatic 200 m buffer that makes neighboring predictions penalty-free. A nearby prediction can earn credit if it covers hidden new-fault truth, and otherwise remains subject to the metric's penalty. Thus reduced off-target mass is a plausible mechanism to test, but these data do not establish that all removed flank cells were useless, nor that pruning caused an authenticated score rise. See the official metric and DrivenData staff clarification.

3. Keep the score records distinct

A saved public participant-board row reports 0.2778 at rank 13 under extradr19; it does not expose a filename or TIFF hash. The owner-supplied H33 README says UNSCORED, while other family filenames and narratives attach differing values to related masks. The H27 0.2708 label likewise lacks a receipt mapping it to the exact 40,199-positive parent bytes. Mirror hashes establish which bytes were compared, not which bytes were uploaded or scored.

Conclusion: “Why did this exact H33 TIFF score 0.2778?” is not answerable from the current evidence because the premise is unauthenticated. A score receipt mapping exact bytes to an organizer result, or a prospectively locked experiment on a defensible holdout, is needed for a causal claim.

4. Relation to current H49 work

H49 is a separate public-proxy research experiment, not a reconstruction of the H33 score. Its SGMC proxy includes non-fault contacts and does not equal the hidden competition target. The shipped H49 arm has a positive descriptive mean against the H33-labelled reference, but the nominal fixed-arm absolute floor does not certify improvement; paired 90% conformal lower bounds on improvement are negative, the final mean rule was selected after outcomes, and prospective timing is not independently verifiable. The H49 slot gate remains closed.
