Executive summary

The objective remains a new, scientifically defensible and unique fault-prediction raster for the DOE GEMS Prize. H47-B tests cross-scale persistence of a rank-encoded upward-continued magnetic channel. The detector is distinct from the historical d-cat pruning artifact, but it did not outperform a random control on the locked spatial blocks. It is not a submission candidate.

The latest saved one-time public-board observation is rank 1 at 0.3774 (participant name not retained); DARD was rank 7 at 0.3195; `extradr19` was rank 13 at 0.2778. The old checked-in 0.3345 rank-1 snapshot is superseded. None of these participant rows authenticates a TIFF-to-score mapping. In particular, the GEMSDOE32 H33-2-B2 file is marked unscored by its owner page.

Do not upload either TIFF in this repository. H47-B failed the preregistered promotion gate. The older d-cat/annulus file is derived by deleting pixels from a published sibling mask and does not meet the no-copy requirement. The old modeled 0.34912 score and 0.34837 “conformal floor” are retired.

What would authorize a future submission?

  1. Obtain and hash authorized competition inputs; verify local mirror provenance against official downloads where possible.
  2. Freeze a spatially blocked baseline and a genuinely new detector, controls, label-coverage rules, and metric before looking at held-out scores.
  3. Require a robust gain over the current spatial holdout best and controls; do not use a slot if that gate fails.
  4. Validate the exact written TIFF and compare it against accessible prior artifacts. State what the uniqueness search cannot prove.
  5. Only after approval, use the current official portal instructions manually with an accurate unique name and short note. See How to submit.
