## Scope and ranking rule

The four future candidates below use the supplied feature stack wherever possible. They target mapped-fault gaps through different physical signatures, not through an unqualified claim that a public proxy equals the hidden labels. Rank is an **ordinal expected gain-per-engineering-cost judgment** based on novelty, data availability, and confounder risk. There is no defensible numeric ΔDTI forecast before spatial holdout testing, so none is invented. A candidate advances only if a preregistered rule beats the current spatial-holdout best, with matched-mass controls and a positive 90% paired-difference lower criterion; ties, unstable folds, or a zero/unsupported lower floor keep the gate closed.

## Evaluated H49 hypotheses (reported separately from the future queue)

### H49-B — signed-polarity scarp coherence: promising proxy mean, not validated

- **Layers:** `det_elev` and `det_elev_slope`; signed across-strike relief at more than one width, with along-strike persistence.
- **Targeted signature:** a consistent up/down polarity along a fault scarp; symmetric terrace, fan or stream edges should be less likely to retain the same sign over the length of a structural trace.
- **Why uncatalogued:** subtle or eroded normal-fault scarps can be absent from the catalogue, while their relief polarity may remain. This is a physical hypothesis, not proof that the feature actually corresponds to a new fault.
- **Novelty:** prior fields were predominantly unsigned gradient/curvature responses; H49-B adds sign consistency. Its result is distinct from H49-A's sampling geometry.
- **Observed H49-B comparison:** at matched spacing/density, R7 polarity field minus the R2 topographic comparison field has mean DTI differences of +0.00642 on the 20-block selection half and +0.00248 on the 19-block calibration half. The R7 field wins 11/20 and 10/19 block pairs. Computed in the R7-minus-R2 direction, the 90% split-conformal lower bounds for a fresh paired block are **−0.00941** on selection and **−0.01621** on calibration. Both remain below zero, so the positive sample means do not establish improvement. These are public Instrument-B proxy results; SGMC includes lithologic contacts and other non-fault traces. This does **not** establish a robust geological improvement or a private score.
- **Cost/access:** already implemented with existing layers. The experimental selection procedure was amended after seeing results; its full adaptive validity is not established. Keep slot gate closed.

### H49-A — strike-aligned sampling geometry: useful negative result, not counted as a geology discovery

The oriented emitter was intended to move matched prediction mass along, rather than across, a lineament. At the tested operating point, oriented-minus-disk mean DTI was −0.01856 on selection and −0.00761 on calibration; it kept fewer blocks than disk (4/20 and 8/19 candidate wins). This does not support a promotion. It is an emission-method control, not a separate geological discovery.

## Validation plan before any future submission slot

1. Restore/verify the exact official-grid features and record source hash, license and access status. Do not depend on an external layer until official free access and actual bytes are verified.
2. State a single physical hypothesis and freeze its transform, candidate rule, mass budget, controls, split, metric and promotion thresholds **before scoring**. Keep the incumbent and a mass-matched randomized/shifted control in the same run.
3. Use spatially contiguous holdout blocks and the entire official footprint contract; do not split neighboring pixels or reuse the same 39 H49 blocks as “new” validation. Record the exchangeability unit (spatial block), target population, sample count, confidence level and order statistic. Block exchangeability remains an assumption, not a proven geological fact.
4. Compare paired block DTI to the spatial-holdout best. Report selection and calibration separately, the mean and distribution of paired differences, and a one-sided 90% split-conformal lower statistic. A positive absolute DTI floor is **not** proof of improvement over the incumbent.
5. Only if the preregistered candidate beats the holdout best on the agreed criterion, controls pass, the exact TIFF/ZIP validates, and the bounded public-inventory uniqueness audit has no exact match may the candidate be reconsidered for a slot. Organizer acceptance and score remain unknown until an official receipt exists.
