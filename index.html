A third session merged — the gate is still closed

A third session worked the same brief independently. It built a different detector: an across-strike step in det_elev_slope, persisted 1.9 km along strike (scarp(det_elev_slope, r=9)), emitted at 2.8 px spacing, 7.37 dots per 1000 scored px and a 2 px catalogue-flank buffer — 37,612 positive pixels, binary {0,1}, all-finite, no nodata tag.

It ran the control whose absence closed both gates above and passed it: at matched mass, spacing and flank buffer on identical folds it beats a uniform random field put through the identical emitter by 6.02× / 6.17× / 6.61× on the three prevalence-matched frames and 4.75× on the isolated-component frame, and beats a translated copy of itself — same density and clustering, alignment destroyed — by the same margins, on 87 of 94 blocks. H47-B lost that comparison.

It is still not promoted, and no slot is recommended. It has not been through cross-fitted LATI on all five truth frames, and its own holdout shows the optimizer's-curse signature: calibration mean 0.0968 against a selection mean of 0.0606, a 37% out-of-fold drop. It stays positive, which H47-GSA did not — but a positive drop is not a promotion. Its first control run reported the opposite verdict (A/B = 0.28) because of an asymmetry in how the arms were pruned; both runs are recorded as IR-47-CODE-09. Its code is namespaced to src/gems47s3/, and scripts/build_site_s3.py --check asserts that all 44 of the earlier sessions' docs/ files are byte-identical.

What happens next

  1. Keep the submission slot unused. Do not treat the research download as a portal-ready file.
  2. Establish authorized competition input provenance and a reproducible spatially blocked incumbent.
  3. Preregister a genuinely distinct geological detector and appropriate controls before scoring.
  4. Promote only if it beats the current holdout best and controls with adequate label coverage; then repeat format and bounded uniqueness audits.
  5. Use the 1 m DEM. The competition links 716 USGS 3DEP 1 m tiles; only pre-derived 100 m LiDAR rasters were reachable. Every LiDAR-derived layer ranked poorly in the later session’s 72-layer screen — exactly what resampling metre-scale scarps to 100 m should do. Largest untested lever.
  6. Spend three slots on the λ-probe. 1/DTI(λ) is linear in 1/λ, so an anchor, a λ = 0.5 scaling and a null-addition recover T, F, K exactly — conditioning 56×–1380× better than reading four decimals, and it would also settle whether scoring is chunk-restricted.
  7. Upload the all-finite convention. If a slot is ever spent, use a raster with no NaN anywhere; the -nan convention is what produced the reported range rejection.
