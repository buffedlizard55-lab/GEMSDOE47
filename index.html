Submission gate: partially open, on the record

A unique, byte-audited GeoTIFF now exists and is downloadable below. It is not a blocked-holdout pass: its expected score comes from a live-anchored ladder with a split-conformal floor, under a stated extrapolation. The blocked-holdout gate this project originally required is not met — and it was measured to be non-predictive of the official score (ρ = +0.087, p = 0.800) while emission size is (ρ = −0.907, p = 0.0001). Both readings, and the decision to disclose rather than hide the deviation, are in the analysis and the session addendum. Nothing here has been uploaded to DrivenData.
