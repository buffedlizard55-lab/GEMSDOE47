What happens next

  1. Keep the competition slot unused. Do not treat any research download as an upload recommendation.
  2. Use official, authorized competition inputs where possible; freeze a reproducible spatially blocked incumbent and control set.
  3. Develop and rank genuinely distinct geological hypotheses, then preregister the detector, holdout, metrics, and controls before scoring.
  4. Promote only if the candidate beats the current holdout best and nontrivial controls with adequate label coverage and a positive, defensible lower bound; repeat exact-byte format and bounded-uniqueness audits.
  5. The official competition description says one submission is selected for scoring across the initial and final prize rounds. Verify the live participant-dashboard rules and deadline; do not assume an unlimited Final Prize Round or spend slots on an unvalidated probe.
  6. The λ-probe could identify metric components, but it is measurement-only and may consume submissions; do not run it unless the organizer's current rules and an authorized participant decide it is worth the opportunity cost.
  7. Follow the current official format instructions rather than guessing about NaN parser behavior. The primary research TIFF uses NaN outside the mirrored sample footprint; the reported historical range-error cause and official footprint remain unresolved, and local validation does not guarantee portal acceptance.
