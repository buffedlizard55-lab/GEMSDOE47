  5. Research the 1 m DEM only after source access and provenance are verified. This checkout used pre-derived 100 m LiDAR layers, which ranked poorly; that result does not establish how native-resolution data would perform.
  6. The λ-probe is not authorized. Its theoretical design uses three score observations; its λ = 1 anchor is owner-reported and not authenticated to a TIFF/score receipt. Three observations do not establish a number of new portal uploads or slots. The accessible official pages checked 2026-10-06 do not state the current per-user quota or slot accounting, so actual upload/slot cost is unknown. Do not call it free or claim a fixed cost. Keep the gate closed unless the portal/organizer confirms the route and a separate diagnostic is preregistered.
  7. Range-error cause unresolved. The rejected raster bytes are unavailable, so do not attribute the reported rejection to NaNs. In the 29 accessible sibling files, 12/12 -nan variants fail one NaN-intolerant local comparison and 17/17 all-finite variants pass it; that demonstrates a possible failure mode, not the cause of the earlier rejection. For any future, separately authorized upload, validate the exact bytes against the current official requirements; the all-finite output writes zeros outside the supplied footprint and does not meet the published null/NaN-outside wording. The NaN variant follows the available mirror convention but fails the tested raw comparison. Neither variant is an upload recommendation; exact portal behavior is unknown.
